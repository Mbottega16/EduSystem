## About Section

### What is EDUSystem?

You are a primary school teacher. Walking the room is the game.

A class of up to eight children sit at desks. Work only advances while you stand at the front teaching it—leave, and the class's attention drains and the work stops. Children get stuck (frozen until you reach them), mess about (spreads to whoever sits next to them), or fall behind on a quiz. You collect finished work, mark homework blind against a hidden answer key, and furnish the room because furniture genuinely helps the child sitting next to it.

A term is 20 days and ends in an exam with a target you can miss. Pass, and the next term carries your room, curriculum, level and coins—but not your class, because the children who passed move up and out. Miss enough times and the school closes.

You can also hire staff, open more rooms, and run the place from a desk. But a room you delegate is simulated by the same honest model you play with your feet.

### The Pitch
*Football Manager for a primary school, played from the inside.*

### Core Features

**Walking is the game.** Every action—teaching, collecting work, marking, shopping, delegating—happens at landmarks you walk to. No hotkey menus, no automation you don't see.

**Furniture matters.** Desks touching a reading corner genuinely help struggling readers. A broken seat in a high-traffic zone costs you real time. The room you build is the room you'll teach in.

**Real consequences.** Work stops when you stop teaching it. Leaving children stuck makes them frustrated and slower. Crowded desks cluster into off-task chats. None of it is scripted; all of it is your choice.

**Grading you can see.** No decimal scores. No hidden stats. A class grades D, C, B, or A based on how well they learned and how fairly you marked them. An exam at term's end is pass-or-fail, and missing too many is game over.

**Two ways to play.**
- **In the room:** Spend your whole career teaching one class, learning the students by heart.
- **At the desk:** Hire staff to run other classrooms while you manage the school from an office. Delegate whole terms to hired teachers—and watch the same simulation that would have happened if you were standing there.

Both are a real way to play, and both are graded the same way.

**Deterministic.** Every run is reproducible. Same day, same seed, same result.

### Target Player

EDUSystem is for players who like:
- **Management sims** where placement and positioning matter (Diner Dash, Overcooked, Football Manager)
- **Strategy** games with real consequences and no undo button
- **Grounded, quiet games** that take their subject seriously
- **Mouse-free gameplay** (keyboard and controller)
- **Games where the rules are fair:** if you lost, you made a choice that cost you

### Playtime

A single term takes 3–5 hours depending on playstyle and classroom state. A full playthrough (school to closure or retirement) is 20–30+ hours.

### Design Principles

- **The room sets the floor, the teacher sets the ceiling.** Furniture helps, but only your teaching gets a class to the top.
- **Legwork, not judgement.** Automation collects things and stacks chairs—it never makes a decision about a child while you're still standing in the room.
- **Prose, not numbers.** You never see a decimal. The game tells you "the room is doing its job," not "0.62."
- **Friction is player-made.** There is no hidden difficulty ramp. A packed room gets harder because you crowded it, not because it's day 15.
- **Walking the room is the game.** Every decision has a physical cost: how far to walk, how long it takes, what you leave unfinished.

### Tone

Grounded, quiet, and genuinely demanding. Not whimsical. Not a joke about school. The children are people. The job is real.

## Content Warnings & Ratings

### Age Rating
**Suitable for 7+**

No violence, blood, or explicit content. Some lessons cover science, social studies, and literature as real subjects. Educational themes only.

### Content Notes

- **Mild frustration themes:** Children in the game can be difficult, slow to learn, or distracted. This reflects real teaching and is part of the game's challenge.
- **No violence, gore, or explicit content**
- **No high-speed flashing or seizure warnings**
- **Colorblind friendly:** All game states use icon/text labels alongside colour
- **Accessibility:** Keyboard rebindable, settings for window/fullscreen, font legible in dark mode

### Difficulty
- No difficulty settings (one curve)
- One mistake per exam can lose you a class
- Solvable on the mechanics alone; no random one-hit-kills

## Notes for Poster/Marketing

**The hook:** "You are a teacher. Walking the room is the game."

**Why it's different:**
- Not a visual novel or dialogue sim about teaching
- Not a casual time-management game
- Real spatial strategy: furniture placement matters, walking takes time, leaving work unfinished has consequences
- Actual simulation: the same model runs delegated rooms, so no "cheap" automation

**Comparison:** If Football Manager is about running a club from the front office, EDUSystem is about standing on the sideline and making it work with your own two feet—and the choice to walk to the office instead.
