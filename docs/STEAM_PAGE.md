## Tagline

**Teach the room. Lose the class. Start again.**

## Short description

EDUSystem is a classroom management sim about the work of teaching. Teach
lessons, walk to stuck pupils, manage disruptions, furnish the room, mark
homework without an answer key, and get a class through its exam. The
children who succeed are the ones who leave.

## Long description

You are the teacher, and the classroom is the game. Stand at the front
and work advances. Leave the teaching position and the room slows down.
Walk between desks to find children who are stuck, deal with pupils who
are disrupting their neighbours, collect finished work and decide where
your attention matters most.

The room is part of the job. Furniture helps the pupils near it, so the
layout you build changes the class you teach. A good room cannot replace
a good teacher, and a good teacher cannot rescue a room that gives the
children nowhere useful to work.

Homework is marked without an answer key. The tells are useful but
imperfect, so every sheet is a judgement rather than a simple answer
check. Quizzes reveal pupils who are falling behind, while unit tests and
the end-of-term exam show whether the work actually held together.

Terms continue, but the class does not. When pupils pass and move on, you
receive a new group. The children you taught best are the ones who leave
you. Keep the school going, improve the room, hire staff when the school
grows, and decide how much of the work you still want to do yourself.

## Features

- **Teach from inside the classroom** — work only advances while you teach.
- **Walk the room** — find stuck pupils, settle disruption and collect
  work yourself.
- **Build a useful classroom** — furniture genuinely changes nearby
  pupils' learning.
- **Mark homework blind** — judge finished work without seeing the answer
  key.
- **Run a career, not a campaign** — pupils move on and every new term
  starts with another class.
